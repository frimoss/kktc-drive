Gastronomic Route

A Taste of Northern Cyprus

Discover the authentic flavours of Northern Cyprus on a scenic road trip through Kyrenia, Bellapais, Lefke and traditional Cypriot villages – hotel to hotel, with lunch and dinner included along the way.

8 Stops Full day · 9–10 hrs ~125 km Kyrenia → Lefke
  1. 1 Kyrenia Harbour
  2. 2 Bellapais Village
  3. 3 Bellapais Abbey
  4. 4 Scenic View Café
  5. 5 Traditional Cypriot Restaurant
  6. 6 Zeytinlik Village
  7. 7 Vuni Palace
  8. 8 Lefke Old Town

1. Kyrenia Harbour

Your journey begins at Kyrenia Harbour, one of the island's most picturesque locations. Once an important trading port, today it is lined with cafés, seafood restaurants, and beautifully restored Venetian buildings. Arrive early to enjoy breakfast by the marina while watching fishing boats return with their morning catch. Fresh seafood, traditional Cypriot coffee, and homemade pastries make this the perfect introduction to the island's culinary traditions.

2. Bellapais Village

Nestled in the Kyrenia Mountains, Bellapais is considered one of Northern Cyprus's most beautiful villages. Narrow cobbled streets lead past traditional stone houses, artisan shops, and quiet cafés with spectacular coastal views. Take your time exploring the village before stopping for freshly squeezed orange juice or traditional Cypriot pastries.

3. Bellapais Abbey

Built during the 13th century, Bellapais Abbey is one of the finest examples of Gothic architecture in the Eastern Mediterranean. The peaceful cloisters and impressive stone halls overlook the coastline, making it one of Northern Cyprus's most photographed landmarks. The nearby cafés provide an ideal place for coffee after your visit.

4. Scenic View Café

After exploring Bellapais, continue to one of the mountain cafés offering panoramic views over Kyrenia and the Mediterranean Sea. Relax with traditional Turkish coffee, homemade lemonade or locally prepared desserts while enjoying one of the island's finest viewpoints.

5. Traditional Cypriot Restaurant

Lunch here is the heart of the day: a proper Cypriot meze, not a quick meal. Expect 15–20 small dishes arriving in waves – halloumi, hummus, village salad, grilled meats and seafood – designed to be shared slowly over conversation. Tell your waiter how many are eating and let the courses come at their own pace; a real meze lunch takes at least two unhurried hours, and few visitors make it to dessert.

6. Zeytinlik Village

Its name literally means "place of olives," and Zeytinlik lives up to it – quiet groves surround the village on every side. Small family producers sell cold-pressed olive oil, olive-oil soap and carob syrup directly from their workshops, often at a fraction of the price you'd pay in a Kyrenia gift shop. It's a short, easy stop, but a genuine one.

7. Vuni Palace

High on a remote hilltop above Morphou Bay, Vouni Palace was built around the 5th century BC, likely for a local ruler sympathetic to Persia. Today the ruins are strikingly empty of tourists, and the view down over the bay is one of the least-photographed panoramas on the island. Time your visit for the hour before sunset, when the light turns the water gold.

8. Lefke Old Town

The day closes in Lefke, a relaxed Ottoman-era town known across the island for its citrus orchards and slower pace of life. Wander the old streets past mosques and colonial-era buildings before settling in for a final dinner – order anything with fresh Lefke oranges, a local specialty found nowhere else in quite the same way.

Insider Tip

Visit Bellapais before 9:30 AM, when the village is still quiet and the morning light perfectly illuminates the abbey. For lunch, choose a family-run village restaurant instead of the tourist-focused waterfront options – many locals consider these the best places to experience authentic meze prepared from recipes passed down through generations. And save Vouni Palace for the hour before sunset: the light over Morphou Bay from up there is the best-kept photo secret on the island.
