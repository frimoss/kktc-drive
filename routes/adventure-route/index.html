Adventure & Entertainment Route

Adrenaline From Nicosia to the West Coast

Karting outside Nicosia, a reef dive, horseback riding, an ATV safari and sunset surfing on the west coast – a full-throttle day across Northern Cyprus, hotel to hotel.

5 Stops Full day · 9–10 hrs ~150 km Nicosia → Yedidalga

Route on the map

1. Cyprus Karting

Start the day at Cemsa Karting & Sporting Center in Ortaköy, on the edge of Nicosia – Northern Cyprus's largest go-kart track. Book the first slot of the morning, before local racing clubs fill the schedule and the tarmac bakes in the midday sun. Ask about the longer endurance sessions if a few quick heats aren't enough.

2. Scuba Diving Center

From Nicosia, head north over the mountains to the coast at Lapta, where Cyprus Underwater Explorers runs reef and wreck dives with decent visibility most of the year. First-timers can usually arrange an introductory dive with an instructor on the spot; certified divers should ask about the deeper wreck sites further along this stretch of coast.

3. Horse Ranche

A short drive further west, The Ranch in Karşıyaka swaps engines for horses – guided rides through the coastal foothills, suited to first-timers and experienced riders alike. It's also a natural lunch stop; the on-site restaurant does a solid, unhurried plate of grilled meat and salad before the afternoon's driving resumes.

4. ATV Safari

Continue west to Gaziveren, where West Coast ATV Safari Tours runs guided quad convoys through the scrubland and dirt tracks above Morphou Bay. It's rougher and dustier than the morning's karting, with the Mediterranean visible on and off through the whole ride – wear something you don't mind getting dirty.

5. Camping and Surfing

The day ends further west still, at Heaven Surfhouse in Yedidalga – one of the west coast's better-known windsurfing and kitesurfing spots, with a laid-back beach bar built for watching the sunset. After a day of engines and adrenaline, this is where the pace finally drops: a swim, a cold drink, and dinner as the sun goes down over Morphou Bay.

Insider Tip

Book Cemsa Karting for the first slot of the morning – locals often reserve blocks of track time by early afternoon, and the tarmac gets uncomfortably hot by midday. Save Heaven Surfhouse for last on purpose: the wind on this stretch of coast tends to build through the afternoon, so late in the day is when the windsurfers and kitesurfers are actually out, and it's also the best angle on the island for a west-facing sunset.
