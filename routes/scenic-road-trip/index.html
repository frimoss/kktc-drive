The Most Scenic Road Trip

From Mountain Castles to the Edge of Cyprus

A mountain village, three castles, a dramatic ridge road and the wild, empty Karpaz Peninsula all the way to Cape Zafer – the island's ultimate drive, hotel to hotel.

9 Stops Full day · 11–12 hrs ~280 km Kyrenia → Karpaz
  1. 1 Bellapais Village
  2. 2 St. Hilarion Castle
  3. 3 Buffavento Scenic Road
  4. 4 Kantara Castle
  5. 5 Karpaz Scenic Road
  6. 6 Golden Beach
  7. 7 Apostolos Andreas Monastery
  8. 8 Cape Zafer
  9. 9 Karpaz Gate Marina

1. Bellapais Village

An early start in the quiet mountain village, before the day's long drive east begins. A quick coffee here sets the pace for a route that spends most of its day behind the wheel, with the payoff being some of the best driving roads on the island.

2. St. Hilarion Castle

The fairytale fortress makes an early, dramatic stop before the road turns east. Even a short visit to the lower courtyards is worth the detour for the view back over the coastline you're about to leave behind.

3. Buffavento Scenic Road

This is less a stop than the drive itself – a genuinely dramatic mountain road that climbs and switchbacks through pine forest along the spine of the Kyrenia range, with the sea appearing and vanishing at every turn. Take it slowly; this stretch is the reason the route earns its name.

4. Kantara Castle

Kantara marks the transition point where the mountain range ends and the long, narrow Karpaz Peninsula begins. It's the last proper sightseeing stop before the drive turns into pure open road for the rest of the day.

5. Karpaz Scenic Road

The peninsula road narrows, traffic thins to almost nothing, and the landscape turns wild and empty – scrubland, stone walls and the occasional roadside donkey. Fuel up before you commit to this stretch; petrol stations become scarce and unreliable past Yenierenköy.

6. Golden Beach

A genuinely wild, dune-backed beach with almost no development, reached by a stretch of unpaved road at the end. It's one of the last true wilderness beaches in the Eastern Mediterranean, and worth the rough final approach.

7. Apostolos Andreas Monastery

A quiet pilgrimage site near the island's easternmost tip, still visited by both Greek and Turkish Cypriots. The stark, empty landscape around it adds to the sense of having reached the edge of somewhere.

8. Cape Apostolos Andreas

The literal end of the road – the northeasternmost point of Cyprus, where the peninsula narrows to a rocky finger and two stretches of sea seem to meet on either side. It's a fitting place to stop the car and just look for a while.

9. Karpaz Gate Marina

Close the long day at this marina development near the base of the peninsula, with dinner overlooking the water before the drive back toward Kyrenia. After a day this long, it's worth the stop before tackling the return journey.

Insider Tip

Fill the tank before you turn onto the Karpaz Peninsula – petrol stations become scarce and unreliable past Yenierenköy, and running low out near Golden Beach with no signal is a genuine problem. Also watch for the peninsula's famous wild donkeys wandering onto the road, especially around dawn and dusk; slow down well before blind corners, locals treat this as routine but visitors are often caught off guard.
